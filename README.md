## 👾 Welcome to the lab

I'm **COMMANDO**, a cybersecurity student and CTF player. This repository documents my journey through **HackerDNA labs**, turning practical challenges into clear, structured write-ups.

The goal is to explain **why an attack works**, how each finding leads to the next step, and what defenders can learn from it.

> 🧠 **Read to understand. Practice to improve. Share what you learn.**

## 🧭 Reading notes

- Start with the lab and try your own approach before opening the solution.
- Expect spoilers for exploitation paths and privilege escalation.
- Replace example addresses with those assigned to your own lab instance.
- Redacted credentials and flags are left out intentionally.
- Practice the techniques only in labs or environments you are authorized to test.
